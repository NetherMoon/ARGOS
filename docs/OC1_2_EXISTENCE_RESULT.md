# OC1.2 measured existence and held-out result

The warm OC1.2 existence search found three complete bids meeting the frozen **at least 8/10** search-panel rule. Its selected bid passed **9/10** of those same ten frozen arrival tables and then passed **28/30 independent fresh arrival/runtime pairs**. The two fresh failures were ResNet QoS only; tracking and Bloom QoS passed all 30. These are tested-scenario counts, not a certified reliability probability.

OC1.2 reused 194 prior development measurements (110 OC-basic v1 and 84 OC1.1 continuation cells) without rerunning them. It executed **147 new FlexDC search calls** across five refinement batches, below its 600-new-call hard cap. New search wall time was **2443.312 seconds** (40.72 minutes), including the last launched batch beyond the 2400-second soft target. The new run measured 40 candidate geometries, of which three received full ten-table panels and 37 were partial; 36 of the new geometries were stopped after their third observed failure. The cumulative state contains 75 measured geometries, 15 complete panels, three eligible bids, and 58 early-rejected bids. The cumulative early-rejection saving is 401 would-be cells; 247 of those savings arose in OC1.2 itself. Aggregate new simulator execution time was 8845.5 seconds, about **36.2%** of ten-worker search capacity; one surviving candidate per batch often left workers idle. Table preparation took 90.515 seconds and V3 candidate-cloud preparation 22.719 seconds, reported separately from search.

The frozen selected bid is `b102-a0-conditional_R-+0.0320`, a legal conditional-reserve probe from the OC1.1 development winner. Its exact vector is Pbar `0.4750245809555053` kW/server, R `0.0625050839114189` kW/server, and weights `[0.25021212339401244, 0.2505734860897064, 0.25269371271133423, 0.2465206927061081]`. It retained 9/10 search support, with its own g8 `-0.03885796742939562`, and reduced mean canonical search objective from the development winner's `91.16466750135938` to **`90.34960266675044`**. The run summary's best g8, `-0.31612038754895844`, belongs to a different eligible bid (`b101-a0-conditional_R--0.0290`); final selection minimizes measured mean objective, not g8. The sole failed search table was seed `1507332087`: p90 `0.010` passed, ResNet Pj `0.17600922722029988` failed. Final selection used the lowest measured mean objective across all ten search tables among the three complete eligible bids; assessment did not influence selection.

The independent assessment used 30 predeclared, disjoint arrival/runtime pairs. All 30 cells completed with valid QoS evidence, 30 distinct initial-table hashes, one grid-signal hash, one target-trace hash, and one frozen candidate geometry. **28/30** passed. Failure pairs were `(arrival 1059138073, runtime 3666771431)` with ResNet Pj `0.10949311320317212`, and `(arrival 3632239313, runtime 863662914)` with ResNet Pj `0.10508067125884146`. No other constraint failed in either cell. Assessment p90 ranged `0.018–0.249` (median `0.1195`), maximum Pj ranged `0–0.109493` (median `0`), and objective ranged `89.291678–91.929268` (median `89.620371`). Across all 30, maximum Bloom Pj was `0.011433`, and the minimum QoS evidence count by job type was `[21081, 18884, 14608, 11903]`.

This supports existence of a common measured 9/10 search-panel bid in the tested domain and substantial descriptive transfer to new scenarios. It does not establish a population reliability bound, certify nearby unevaluated geometry, or prove that a 10/10 point exists. The remaining measured vulnerability is ResNet QoS. The source commit recorded in the run manifest is `36989f08e9325e9475e506db14e149007398e539`; source hashes were verified against that working version before the subsequent OC1.3 scheduling change.
