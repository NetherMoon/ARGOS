# ARGOS-OC1.1 frozen search protocol

OC-basic v1 remains unchanged. OC1.1 is a separate, measured ten-arrival protocol for `W2-short-qos5_4.5_4_3.5`, N=1000, U=0.6. The same ten frozen initial job tables and one fixed search runtime seed are used. A complete bid must pass at least eight of ten tables under the unchanged p90 <= 0.30, every Pj <= 0.10, and evidence-validity rules. Only a complete ten-table bid may be selected. Selection among eligible bids minimizes mean canonical objective across **all ten** tables.

For each scenario, signed normalized violation is the maximum of `p90/0.30 - 1` and each `Pj/0.10 - 1`; nonpositive means that scenario passes. For complete but ineligible bids, ranking is lower eighth-smallest signed violation (g8), then lower sum of the smallest failures needed to reach eight passes, then more passes, then lower all-ten objective. The ninth and tenth worst scenarios therefore do not dominate the repair ranking. The highest-ranked complete measured bid and a geometrically distinct high-support bid anchor refinements. Partial bids can suggest centers but cannot win or receive exact g8.

The cold initial screen has 15 bids: 12 distinct V3-guided candidates selected cyclically by predicted margin, normalized violation, and objective among predicted-feasible points, plus three distinct V3-scored independent candidates. Each refinement batch has eight bids: three structured probes around each of two measured anchors, plus two independent candidates. Each anchor receives a weight/combined probe, a legal normalized Pbar probe, and a legal conditional-reserve probe. The sign alternates by batch and anchor; V3 predictions cannot veto a measured-anchor probe. The standard ARGOS controller and FlexDC source are unchanged.

The scenario order is frozen before OC1.1 measurements from the OC-basic v1 seed-difficulty CSV: v1 pass count ascending, minimum measured signed violation descending, numeric seed ascending. The frozen order is `2381098110, 1507332087, 2495920665, 2720817509, 2812640469, 422443260, 3463391848, 3385754323, 598775175, 1841060571`. Within each candidate batch, the scheduler evaluates this order in deterministic rounds using one persistent pool of at most ten workers. After three measured failures, it launches no more scenarios for that bid. All completed calls remain recorded. Proposal generation occurs after a batch finishes, so worker completion order cannot alter the next proposal batch.

Development continuation reuses the 11 complete OC-basic v1 candidate panels as prior measured development evidence, adds no more than 250 new calls, and has a 1200-second additional search soft target. It is **not** a clean benchmark and receives no held-out assessment. OC1.2 is a separate development/existence search with a 2400-second soft target and 600-new-call hard cap; a qualifying bid receives 30 predeclared held-out arrival/runtime assessments without feedback. OC1.3 uses fresh FlexDC candidate evaluations from clean cold starts under the same scientific OC1.1 logic: first 1200 seconds, then 600 seconds. The finite panel and held-out counts are descriptive, not certified reliability probabilities.

Scientific-source SHA256 hashes, source commit, table and grid hashes, seed panel/order, v1 and Phase 2B provenance, worker limit, time target, and new-call cap are frozen in each run manifest. An interrupted run can resume only with exactly matching identity; completed exact cells are reused. An incomplete or mismatched cell aborts for review.
