# ARGOS-OC OC1.1–OC1.3 research interpretation

This is a finite-panel study for W2-short-qos5_4.5_4_3.5, N=1000, U=0.6. A complete bid passes the search rule when at least eight of the same ten frozen arrival tables pass p90 <= 0.30 and every Pj <= 0.10 with valid evidence. A pass count is not a reliability probability. The cold timing runs used source commit `420733331f8e84d9249611aa1238a48aeb0c0281`, ten FlexDC workers, no imported simulator observations, and the same frozen tables, grid, and search runtime seed. Their initial job-table hashes and their selected bid's ten per-seed metrics match exactly.

The complete numeric comparison is in `oc_stage_comparison.csv`. “Cold” means no simulator result reuse. Both timing runs reused the verified, job-table-independent historical V3 bank. They each regenerated the ten initial job tables and reconstructed/scored the V3 candidate cloud. Table preparation took 90.64/88.78 seconds and cloud work 21.92/23.59 seconds (cold20/cold10), separately from search. The reused bank originally took 350.65 seconds to generate on CPU; that cost was **not incurred** in either timing run. Measured end-to-end invocation times with the bank ready were 1343.19 and 735.47 seconds. These are laptop CPU measurements, not a GPU production timing claim.

## Why v1 stopped at 5/10

**Measured:** v1 spent 100 of 110 calls on ten full initial panels before its first adaptive result. It generated six second-batch proposals but evaluated only one before the 20-minute soft target. Its 5/10 best bid had four large Bloom failures and one tracking failure. The initial full-panel budget allocation delayed adaptive feedback; its pass-count ranking and broad multi-axis local probe did not target the signed eight-of-ten boundary well. The same ten tables later admitted a 9/10 measured bid after a legal 0.01 ResNet-to-Bloom weight transfer from that 5/10 bid. Thus the v1 outcome was a search-protocol failure on this panel, not evidence that the eight-of-ten target was unattainable. The separate effects of time, ranking, screening, and probe design cannot be isolated from these non-factorial runs.

## What the revisions achieved

**Measured:** OC1.1 introduced a signed scenario violation and complete-panel g8, early rejection after three observed failures, smaller structured probes, and multiple measured anchors. Its warm continuation reached 9/10 at new call 55. OC1.2 retained that development evidence, found three complete eligible bids, and selected the lowest mean canonical objective among them. The selected 9/10 bid passed 28 of 30 predeclared fresh arrival/runtime pairs. Both fresh failures were ResNet QoS (Pj 0.109493 and 0.105081); tracking and Bloom passed all 30. Its own search-panel g8 was -0.038858. The more negative best g8 in the OC1.2 summary (-0.316120) belongs to a different eligible bid; objective, not g8, determined selection.

The two independent cold timing runs each discovered the **same** 8/10 bid, `b01-screen-10`, directly in the 15-candidate initial V3-guided screen. It is a V3 snapshot, not a measured adaptive-refinement product. Its vector is Pbar 0.477241575717926, R 0.0753170251846313, weights [0.25302305817604065, 0.2478901892900467, 0.24925294518470764, 0.24983380734920502]. Its two failed search tables were seed 3385754323 (tracking p90 0.390) and seed 3463391848 (GPT2 Pj 0.152546). The eighth-best normalized margin was only 0.016667 (g8 -0.016667). No later cold20 proposal improved on this result; cold10 ended after its first batch. Neither cold bid received a held-out assessment, so the 28/30 transfer result applies to the different OC1.2 9/10 bid only.

**Timing:** cold20 first qualified at 563.7 search seconds, within ten minutes of the search clock, and stopped at 1230.6 seconds after its last launched wave. Cold10 first qualified at 623.0 seconds and stopped at 623.1 seconds. Thus the strict ten-minute search target was **missed by 23.0 seconds** in the dedicated cold10 run, although the predeclared soft limit correctly let already-launched work finish. The twenty-minute target was met for first discovery, not for total end-to-end invocation including setup. One run at each deadline cannot establish timing repeatability.

Early rejection left 29/30 non-winning cold20 candidates and 14/15 cold10 candidates partial. It avoided 180 and 94 potential panel cells, respectively, relative to completing those early-rejected panels. The cross-candidate scheduler filled all ten worker slots at peak and achieved aggregate simulator-time / ten-worker-search-time proxies of 75.6% and 71.8%, versus 36.2% in OC1.2. These are descriptive throughput figures; the runs also differ in prior evidence, candidate mix, and stopping horizon, so they do not attribute a causal speedup to one engineering change. Controller scheduling overhead within the search clock was not instrumented separately from worker idle and result-journal time. The cold20 run made 72 more calls after the first eligible bid without finding a better eligible candidate.

## Research conclusion and limits

**Verified:** a common measured eight-of-ten bid exists on the frozen panel and can be found from a clean simulator search without reusing earlier FlexDC observations. Structured warm refinement found nine-of-ten bids, and one selected nine-of-ten bid transferred to 28/30 fresh pairs. The original v1 5/10 result mainly reflected its measured allocation and proposal limitations; the cold result also shows V3 prescreening can itself surface an eligible point. **Interpretation:** the feasible geometry is sensitive to small weight and reserve changes, and the cold bid has a narrow eighth-scenario margin. The available observations do not determine whether the robust region is broad, narrow, or disconnected. Nor do they establish smooth, learnable local residuals or a population reliability guarantee.

The aborted `argos_oc_1_3_cold20_20260926T191700Z` attempt is excluded from timing: a transient Windows receipt-file lock interrupted it after 50 raw FlexDC executions (42 journaled rows). A bounded retry for receipt replacement was committed before both clean runs; it did not change candidate generation, feasibility, or selection. The frozen v1 archive and standard ARGOS controller remain unchanged.

Evidence: the five run `summary.json` files, `analysis_summary.json` for OC1.1–OC1.3, their `search/candidate_states.csv` and scenario CSVs, the OC1.2 `final/fresh_assessment_results.csv`, the two cold `arrival_panel/table_manifest.csv` files, and `oc_stage_comparison.csv`. The cold runs also contain `figures/` for progress, geometry, and candidate-by-table outcomes.
