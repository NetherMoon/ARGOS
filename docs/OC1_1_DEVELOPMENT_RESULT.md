# OC1.1 development continuation result

The versioned OC1.1 search found a bid that passed **9/10** of the frozen W2 N1000 U0.6 arrival tables. This is development evidence only. No fresh held-out arrival/runtime scenarios were used.

The initial OC-basic v1 evidence consisted of 11 complete bids, 110 FlexDC calls, and a best support of 5/10. The corrected OC1.1 continuation added **84** FlexDC calls over **1219.25 seconds** of search time (1334.25 seconds including frozen-table preparation and V3 cloud work). The first >=8/10 complete-panel bid appeared at **new call 55**, **769.204 seconds** into new search. It was also the final winner. The search ended at its predeclared soft wall target, with 12 complete panels including the 11 v1 panels, 23 partial panels, 22 early-rejected bids, and one eligible bid. Early rejection saved 154 would-be table evaluations relative to completing every early-rejected panel. Approximate worker utilization was **40.6%**, with a measured peak of eight active workers; batches with one surviving candidate still left worker capacity idle.

The selected bid `b04-a1-weight-0.01` is a measured `targeted_weight_transfer` from the original 5/10 candidate `b01-initial-01`. It changed only the four scheduling weights, moving **0.01 from ResNet to Bloom**. Pbar remained `0.4750245809555053` kW/server and R remained `0.0537046119570732` kW/server. New weights are `[0.25021212339401244, 0.2505734860897064, 0.25269371271133423, 0.2465206927061081]`. Its mean canonical objective over all ten tables was **91.16466750135938**; g8 was **-0.3049835731803243**. It passed nine tables. The single failed table was arrival seed `1507332087`, with tracking p90 `0.011` and ResNet Pj `0.28604382929642447`. All ten executions had valid evidence and fixed table/grid identities. This result does not establish reliability on unseen arrival tables.

The probe is scientifically informative: a small, legal weight transfer moved the measured search-panel support from 5/10 to 9/10 without changing Pbar or R. The old dominant Bloom failures disappeared on this selected bid, while one ResNet QoS failure remained. This is consistent with a sensitive scheduling tradeoff, not proof of a broad or smooth common-feasibility region. The result motivates the predeclared OC1.2 existence search and independent assessment; it does not justify a GP or a claim about population reliability yet.
